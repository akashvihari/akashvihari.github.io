--- name: "Eastern Ghats & Coastal Andhra Explorer" description: "An evocative, editorial travel and cultural tourism design system celebrating Visakhapatnam coastal elegance, Araku misty valleys, Kasipatnam waterfalls, Paderu tribal coffee highlands, and Vizianagaram royal heritage." --- ## Overview The Eastern Ghats & Coastal Andhra Explorer design system evokes the misty serenity of Araku's coffee plantations, the deep azure waves of Visakhapatnam's Bay of Bengal coastline, the rustic earthen red hues of tribal handicraft traditions, and the majestic stone architecture of Vizianagaram's Fort. The interface combines an editorial magazine layout with high-utility travel logistics, immersive destination previews, elevation waypoints, and itinerary planning tools.
